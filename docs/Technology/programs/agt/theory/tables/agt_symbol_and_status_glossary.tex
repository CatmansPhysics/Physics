\section*{AGT Symbol and Status Glossary}

This table normalizes the notation and statement-status labels for the AGT program. It is the canonical reference for the clean theory manuscript and for the computational manuscript.

\subsection*{Statement Status Labels}

\begingroup
\small
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{3pt}
\begin{longtable}{@{}L{0.14\linewidth}L{0.82\linewidth}@{}}
\textbf{Label} & \textbf{Meaning} \\
{[A]} & Definition or axiom adopted as part of the AGT program language. \\
{[D]} & Derived proposition expected to follow from the imported framework together with AGT-specific axioms. \\
{[T]} & Target theorem not yet claimed as proved; used for scaling, compatibility, and existence claims. \\
{[H]} & Working hypothesis used conditionally in later sections. \\
{[IA]} & Imported effective ansatz used explicitly as non-core continuum language. \\
F & Failure mode that identifies a concrete collapse mechanism of the program. \\
